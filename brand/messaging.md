# ten forty five — master messaging playbook
Version 1.0 | Workshop 04 | Approved strategy; sample copy is draft unless identified as approved.

## Identity and positioning (approved)
Marketing & Technology Consultancy. Official tagline: **your business. our expertise.** Marketing at our core, business growth as our purpose, expertise without ego. Human-led, AI-enabled. Company-led public identity, founder-led client experience; no public founder names, photos, employer references or home address at launch without approval.

## Core message (approved)
You know your business. We know how to help. We help businesses get noticed, find more customers and make everyday work easier. Whatever you're working toward, we bring the marketing and technology expertise to help you get there.

Mission: To make professional marketing and technology expertise more accessible to businesses, without the need for a full-time hire or a long-term agency commitment.

Vision: We believe every business should have access to great marketing, smart technology and experienced people, regardless of its size.

Promise: We make it easy to get the right help for your business. Clear pricing, thoughtful recommendations, quality work and a process that respects your time.

## Narrative
Access is why we exist; complexity is what we simplify; execution is what we deliver. Publicly speak to business needs, not industries. Internally target specific industries. Four customer needs: getting started, finding more customers, improving what exists, getting time back. Clients retain decisions and ownership of paid deliverables, subject to third-party licenses. No mandatory retainers or lock-in. Start with the problem, recommend a solution, agree scope and price, execute.

## Voice rules
Conversational professionalism: clear, confident, thoughtful, personable, understated. Website/ads: modern and subtly witty (3/5), occasional 4/5. Proposals and client communications: polished, direct, warm. Educational content: useful and accessible. Barbecue test: a doctor, tradesperson or teenage intern should understand it on first hearing. We speak to people, not job titles. Use natural sentences, contractions where appropriate, concrete outcomes before technical terms. Never talk down, force jokes, use corporate jargon, or promise results without evidence. Avoid partner as default descriptor. Say find more customers rather than lead generation; make everyday work easier rather than workflow optimization; turn visitors into customers rather than conversion optimization. Explain AI as a tool with human judgment and review, not a replacement for expertise.

## Commercial facts (approved)
- do it yourself. — digital products: build your website. $19; market your business. $29; work smarter. $49. Paid upfront, no upgrade credits.
- get started. — $1,500, one focused project; $750 upfront and $750 before handoff.
- think bigger. — $3,500, larger/complex project or connected improvements; $1,750 upfront and $1,750 before handoff. Replaces do more.
- let's talk. — custom scope and pricing after discovery; ongoing work optional.
Fixed packages: direct purchase or optional free call, subject to scope confirmation. No mandatory subscription or optional add-on menu. Third-party costs disclosed separately. Review prices after first five paid projects. Exact deliverables and timelines pending validation.

## Master introduction (draft wording, approved narrative)
We're ten forty five, a marketing and technology consultancy built to make professional expertise more accessible to businesses. We help businesses reach more customers, build better websites and make smarter use of technology. Not every business needs a full-time team or a long-term agency contract. We get to know your business, recommend the right way forward and do the work.

## Copy review
Check comprehension, accuracy, client agency, scope, price, tone, evidence, privacy and clear next action. Never invent testimonials, results, credentials, founder details or guarantees. Approved visual identity lives in the founder-approved brand kit; exact assets not verified here. Do not infer colors or fonts from drafts.
