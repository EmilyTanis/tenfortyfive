## 01: Website's main job (approved)

**Inform, then sell.** At launch, visitors arrive from ads and personalized LinkedIn outreach. They want to learn what we do and what it costs; they are not ready to buy on arrival.

- Every page answers quickly: *what do you do, what does it cost, how do I start.*
- Primary action: **Book a free call.**
- Secondary action: **See pricing.** Pricing is public and one click from anywhere.
- Direct purchase stays available on the pricing page (approved in Workshop 02/04) but is not the headline action.

### Site principle: no gates (approved)

Visitors have already been contacted once (ad or personal outreach); the site is their second touch point. It gives answers before it asks for anything.

- No pop-ups: no email-capture modals, exit-intent pop-ups or chat widgets that open on their own.
- Nothing gated: pricing, package details, process and FAQs are readable without a form or sign-up.
- Short booking: name, email and one optional "what are you working on?" field. Everything else is covered on the call.
- Links land on the answer: outreach and ad links go to the page that matches what the message promised, not always the homepage.
- Cookie consent only as legally required, shown as a small bar, never a blocking overlay.

**Changes to the draft copy brief:** homepage CTAs become *Book a free call* (primary) and *See pricing* (secondary), replacing *Find the right help* and *See our work*. A guided "find the right help" picker can be added after 10–20 real sales conversations show which questions sort people well.

## 02: Sitemap and navigation (approved)

Soft-launch menu, five pages, labels kept plain so anyone understands them at a glance:

| Page | Job |
|---|---|
| **Home** | What we do in one scroll, pricing at a glance, book a call |
| **What we do** | Websites, marketing, AI & business systems, each tied to the four customer needs from Workshop 04 |
| **Pricing** | All four packages, what's included, how payment works, FAQ |
| **About** | Why ten forty five exists; human-led, AI-enabled; no founder names or photos at launch |
| **Book a call** | Short form plus calendar booking |

- Footer: Privacy and Terms (required once we take payments or collect form data).
- Unlisted: one landing page per ad campaign, matching that ad's message.
- Added later: **Work** once the landscaping site is live (until then, clearly labeled concept projects may appear on Home); **Shop / Resources** once the do it yourself. products are ready.

**Changes to the draft copy brief:** navigation drops Work, Resources and Contact at launch (Book a call replaces Contact).

## 03: Homepage structure (approved)

Sell the value first, confirm the price second. Within seconds of landing, a visitor should see what we do, how we work and what their business gets out of it. Pricing is visible and honest but is not the homepage's main visual feature.

| # | Section | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opening | *your business. our expertise.* plus one plain line on what a business gets from us. **Book a free call**; small "see pricing" text link beneath |
| 2 | What you get | Outcomes, not services: more customers finding you, a website that does its job, time back from busywork, a clear plan for what to do next (the four Workshop 04 needs, framed as results) |
| 3 | What we do | Strengths (websites, marketing, AI & business systems), each with 2–3 concrete examples a business owner would recognize. Examples are draft until package contents are confirmed |
| 4 | How we work | Four steps (tell us what you need → agree on work and price → we do the work → you own the result) plus how it feels: plain language, quick replies, people making the decisions with AI helping |
| 5 | Why ten forty five | You own everything, no long contracts, clear prices up front |
| 6 | Pricing | One compact row: four packages and prices, linking to Pricing |
| 7 | Closing | Book a free call |

- No testimonials or client-logo section until real, permitted proof exists; a Work section is added once the landscaping site is live.
- FAQ lives on the Pricing page, not the homepage.
