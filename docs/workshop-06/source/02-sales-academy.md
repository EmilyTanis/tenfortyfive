# ten forty five. | Sales Academy Workbook
Learn • Apply • Practice • Demonstrate

## 01 — Know our business
Learn: We sit beside owners, not above them. Human-led, AI-enabled means people own judgment and quality while technology supports delivery. Explain value without jargon or unsupported claims.
Apply: Write a 30-second introduction for a business owner unfamiliar with marketing.
Practice prompt: Using approved repository documents, role-play a skeptical local owner. Ask what ten forty five. does and why it differs from an agency. Give feedback after the conversation.
Checklist: explain purpose, three service areas, human oversight, value and promise boundaries naturally.

## 02 — Know what we sell
Learn: Websites help customers find and contact businesses; marketing helps attract and measure demand; AI and technology can simplify repetitive workflows. Learn the approved four packages and pricing from the current catalog. Recognize needs without pretending to build the solution.
Apply: Match a slow inquiry form, low awareness and manual appointment reminders to possible service areas. Ask two clarifying questions for each.
Practice prompt: Act as an owner describing frustrations without jargon. Let me identify opportunities and ask questions; do not reveal the answer prematurely.
Checklist: distinguish service areas, explain packages accurately, identify opportunities, escalate technical scope.

## 03 — Find the right businesses
Learn: Research new and established businesses equally. Use public directories, maps, LinkedIn and chambers. Evaluate need, fit, access and readiness. Separate observation from hypothesis.
Apply: Research five real businesses. For each record source, observation, hypothesis, decision-maker, fit and pursue/hold/pass rationale. Do not contact them during training.
Practice prompt: Review my five research records against approved criteria; challenge assumptions and ask me to defend one decision not to pursue.
Checklist: verify sources, identify relevant contacts, qualify and disqualify responsibly.

## 04 — Start meaningful conversations
Learn: Personalize without pressure. Use the approved four-touch cadence. Adapt to email, LinkedIn and phone. Stop on opt-out. Generic: We can revolutionize your business. Better: I noticed your booking process requires a phone call; how is that working for your team?
Apply: Write an email, LinkedIn message and phone opener for three different scenarios.
Practice prompt: Give me three distinct business scenarios, evaluate my messages for accuracy, relevance, natural tone and respectful next steps.
Checklist: use verified observations, clear language, low-pressure calls to action and approved cadence.

## 05 — Listen before recommending
Learn: Discover goals, customer journey, existing investments, bottlenecks, budget and timing. A request for ads may conceal a conversion problem. Recommend the right solution, not the most expensive one.
Apply: Prepare five open questions and a summary for an owner asking for more advertising.
Practice prompt: Role-play an owner requesting ads whose underlying problem may be website conversion. Reveal details only when asked; evaluate listening and judgment.
Checklist: ask open questions, distinguish symptoms from causes, discuss budget respectfully, summarize and escalate.

## 06 — Sales readiness and professional judgment
Learn: Handle objections calmly, use approved pricing, avoid guarantees, log activity in HubSpot and establish next steps. Technical scope, discounts and commitments require approval.
Apply: Respond to objections about cost, AI, existing agency and results. Write a sample CRM record and technical handoff.
Practice prompt: Role-play an owner with multiple objections. Challenge my proposed next step and evaluate accuracy, trust and professional follow-through.
Checklist: handle objections, recommend appropriately, document clearly, identify approval boundaries and know when to walk away.

## Certification
Complete all six modules, then pass three independent simulations: introduction, discovery and skeptical customer. Each requires 85/100 or higher and no critical errors. See AI coaching instructions for scoring and retakes. Certification permits live outreach, not independent approval of technical scope, discounts or commitments.
