# Sales & Business Development Playbook
Internal reference · Workshop 06 · Draft v1, reconciled November 25, 2026

## Positioning
**your business. our expertise.** You know your business. We know how to help. We help businesses get noticed, find more customers and make everyday work easier, through human-led, AI-enabled work: people own the judgment and the quality, and technology helps us work faster.

Our philosophy: *we sit next to you, not above you.* The owner keeps the decisions and ownership. Our job is to earn the trust of the right customers, not to win every customer. Recommend what is right for the business, not what is worth most to us.

Every message passes the barbecue test: a doctor, a tradesperson or a teenage intern should understand it the first time they hear it.

## Services and boundaries
- **Websites and digital experiences:** help customers find, use and contact the business, on any device.
- **Marketing and advertising:** help the right people discover the business and choose it, and measure what works.
- **AI and business systems:** make repeatable work easier, with a person always checking.

Technical scope requires approval from the technical founder. Never invent package names, rates, discounts, guarantees or delivery dates.

## The four packages (approved)
| Package | Price | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| do it yourself. | $19 / $29 / $49 | The owner would rather do it themselves with a guide: build your website., market your business., work smarter. Instant checkout, 14-day refund. |
| get started. | $1,500 | They know the one problem they want solved. Typically 2–3 weeks, 2 rounds of changes, 7 days of support. |
| think bigger. | $3,500 | Something has to change, but they're not sure exactly what: a bigger goal, usually several connected problems. Starts with a planning step. Typically 3–5 weeks, 3 rounds of changes, 30 days of support plus a check-in. |
| let's talk. | Custom | Unusual, extensive or ongoing needs. Priced after discovery. |

- Fixed packages are paid half to start and half before handoff. No subscriptions, no add-on menu, no upgrade credits.
- Buying is **tell us, then pay**: a four-question project form or a free call. Within one business day we send the confirmed scope, timeline and a payment link.
- Third-party costs (hosting, ad spend, software) are disclosed before commitment.
- In outreach, never promise specific deliverables. The deliverable is agreed at scope confirmation.

## Prospecting
- Give new and established businesses equal attention, in New Jersey first.
- Research Google Maps, LinkedIn, chambers of commerce, directories and announcements.
- Record the business, source URL, observed opportunity, hypothesis, decision-maker, fit and next step. Separate evidence from assumptions.
- Qualify need, fit, decision-maker access and readiness to invest. Disqualify poor fit, unrealistic expectations or unwillingness to engage.
- Collect only the business information you need. Prospect records live in HubSpot or the restricted Drive folder, never in GitHub.

## Outreach
**Channels.** Email and LinkedIn are the starting channels. Phone is used only where it is appropriate and welcome (see the open items).

**Sequence.** Use the approved scripts from `sales/playbook.md`: a first message and two follow-ups. Stop on an opt-out or a clear decline. Personalize every message with one verified observation.

- **Email:** Hi [name], I noticed [specific observation]. We help businesses improve [relevant outcome]. Would you be open to a short conversation about how you're approaching that today?
- **LinkedIn:** Hi [name], I came across [business] and was curious about [specific business question].
- **Phone (where appropriate):** Hi, I'm with ten forty five. Is now a reasonable time for a quick question about [topic]?

Use an accurate sender identity, honor opt-outs and comply with applicable outreach rules, including CAN-SPAM for commercial email. No pressure, unsupported claims or mass generic messaging. No live outreach before certification and founder approval.

## Discovery
- Ask about business goals, the current customer journey, existing marketing, bottlenecks, how they measure success, budget and timing.
- Listen, summarize and confirm. For example, if someone wants ads, ask how current visitors become inquiries before recommending spend.
- Explain options in plain language and agree on the next step.
- Escalate technical questions and scope to the technical founder. Do not quote custom prices or timelines.

## Objections and proposals
- **Price:** ask what the prospect is comparing, and clarify scope. Never offer an unauthorized discount. Point self-directed owners to do it yourself.
- **AI:** explain human oversight and limits. A real person makes the decisions, reviews the work and is accountable for delivery.
- **Existing agency:** respect the relationship and explore complementary needs.
- **Results:** discuss how we will measure them. Never guarantee outcomes.
- **Credibility:** use only verified examples.
- **Ownership:** the client owns paid custom deliverables and their accounts, subject to third-party licensing. No mandatory maintenance.

Proposals must reflect the approved scope, price, responsibilities and terms. No unauthorized commitments.

## HubSpot and handoff
- **Record:** source, company, contact, consent and opt-out, observed need, qualification, stage, last touch, next action and owner.
- **Proposed stages:** researched, contacted, engaged, qualified, discovery, proposal, won/lost. These map to the tracking list in `sales/playbook.md`.
- Log follow-ups and reasons for loss.
- **Handoff to the technical founder** includes goals, scope assumptions, budget, timeline, stakeholders and unresolved risks.
- **Weekly pipeline review:** qualified opportunities, replies, discovery meetings, proposals, wins and next actions.
- **Primary measure:** one qualified meeting booked per week, reviewed after 30 days.

Certification is mandatory before live outreach.
