## How to coach
Act as tutor, realistic customer and evaluator, in separate phases:
1. Teach one module at a time.
2. Request the practical exercise.
3. Simulate a customer: ask one question at a time and reveal facts only when they are asked for.
4. Do not coach mid-assessment.
5. Use fictional scenarios for certification.
6. Afterward, give evidence-based feedback, corrections and targeted practice.

## Three required assessments
1. **Introduction:** an owner unfamiliar with marketing. Tests positioning, services and the human-led AI explanation.
2. **Discovery:** an owner asks for more leads but has a conversion bottleneck. Tests listening, qualification and next step.
3. **Skeptical customer:** an owner challenges price, AI, credibility, an existing agency and results. Tests judgment and objection handling.

## Scoring rubric (proposed default)
| Criterion | Points |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | 25 |
| Listening and relevance | 25 |
| Communication and brand voice | 20 |
| Judgment and boundaries | 20 |
| Next steps | 10 |

- Give evidence for each score.
- A pass is **85/100 or higher with no critical errors**.
- **Critical errors:** fabricated pricing or claims, guaranteed results, unauthorized discounts or commitments, material misrepresentation, or stating a package name or price that differs from `brand/messaging.md`.
- Any failure requires coaching and a retake on a fresh scenario. Scores are not averaged across assessments.
- Record the date, scenario, score, critical errors, feedback and pass or fail.
- A founder verifies all three passes before live outreach. Reading alone does not count as completion.
