# Sales Academy Workbook
Learn · Apply · Practice · Demonstrate · Workshop 06 · Draft v1, reconciled November 25, 2026

How to use this workbook: work through one module at a time. For each module, read **Learn**, complete **Apply** in writing, run the **Practice prompt** with an AI coach (see `03-ai-coaching.md`), then tick the **Checklist**. Use only fictional or public information. Do not contact any business during training.

## 01 · Know our business
**Learn.** Our tagline is *your business. our expertise.* You know your business; we know how to help. *We sit next to you, not above you.* Human-led, AI-enabled means people own the judgment and quality while technology supports delivery. Explain value without jargon or unsupported claims, and pass the barbecue test.

**Apply.** Write a 30-second introduction for a business owner who knows nothing about marketing.

**Practice prompt.** Using the approved repository documents, role-play a skeptical local owner. Ask what ten forty five does and how it differs from an agency. Give feedback after the conversation.

**Checklist.**
- [ ] Explains our purpose naturally
- [ ] Names the three service areas
- [ ] Explains human oversight
- [ ] States the value without over-promising

## 02 · Know what we sell
**Learn.** Websites help customers find and contact a business. Marketing helps attract and measure demand. AI and business systems simplify repetitive work. Learn the four packages exactly:

| Package | Price | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| do it yourself. | $19 / $29 / $49 | They'd rather do it themselves with a guide |
| get started. | $1,500 | They know the one problem they want solved |
| think bigger. | $3,500 | Something has to change, but they're not sure exactly what |
| let's talk. | Custom | Unusual, extensive or ongoing needs |

Fixed packages are half to start and half before handoff. The deliverable is agreed at scope confirmation, so recognize needs without pretending to design the solution.

**Apply.** Match a slow inquiry form, low awareness and manual appointment reminders to possible service areas and packages. Ask two clarifying questions for each.

**Practice prompt.** Act as an owner describing frustrations without jargon. Let me identify opportunities and ask questions; do not reveal the answer too early.

**Checklist.**
- [ ] Distinguishes the service areas
- [ ] States package names and prices accurately
- [ ] Identifies opportunities
- [ ] Escalates technical scope

## 03 · Find the right businesses
**Learn.** Research new and established New Jersey businesses equally, using public directories, maps, LinkedIn and chambers of commerce. Evaluate need, fit, decision-maker access and readiness to invest. Separate observation from hypothesis.

**Apply.** Research five real businesses. For each, record the source, observation, hypothesis, decision-maker, fit, and a pursue, hold or pass rationale. Do not contact them during training. Keep records in the restricted Drive folder, not GitHub.

**Practice prompt.** Review my five research records against the approved criteria. Challenge my assumptions and ask me to defend one decision not to pursue.

**Checklist.**
- [ ] Verifies sources
- [ ] Identifies the relevant contacts
- [ ] Qualifies and disqualifies responsibly

## 04 · Start meaningful conversations
**Learn.** Personalize without pressure. Use the approved sequence: a first message and two follow-ups, from `sales/playbook.md`. Adapt to email and LinkedIn, and to phone where appropriate. Stop on opt-out.
- **Generic:** "We can revolutionize your business."
- **Better:** "I noticed your booking process requires a phone call. How is that working for your team?"

**Apply.** Write an email, a LinkedIn message and a phone opener for three different scenarios.

**Practice prompt.** Give me three distinct business scenarios. Evaluate my messages for accuracy, relevance, natural tone and a respectful next step.

**Checklist.**
- [ ] Uses verified observations
- [ ] Uses clear language
- [ ] Uses low-pressure calls to action
- [ ] Follows the approved sequence

## 05 · Listen before recommending
**Learn.** Discover goals, the customer journey, existing investments, bottlenecks, budget and timing. A request for ads may hide a conversion problem. Recommend the right solution, not the most expensive one.

**Apply.** Prepare five open questions and a summary for an owner asking for more advertising.

**Practice prompt.** Role-play an owner requesting ads whose underlying problem may be website conversion. Reveal details only when asked. Evaluate my listening and judgment.

**Checklist.**
- [ ] Asks open questions
- [ ] Separates symptoms from causes
- [ ] Discusses budget respectfully
- [ ] Summarizes and escalates

## 06 · Sales readiness and professional judgment
**Learn.** Handle objections calmly, use approved pricing, avoid guarantees, log activity in HubSpot and set next steps. Technical scope, discounts and commitments require approval.

**Apply.** Respond to objections about cost, AI, an existing agency and results. Write a sample CRM record and a technical handoff.

**Practice prompt.** Role-play an owner with several objections. Challenge my proposed next step and evaluate my accuracy, trust and professional follow-through.

**Checklist.**
- [ ] Handles objections
- [ ] Recommends appropriately
- [ ] Documents clearly
- [ ] Knows the approval boundaries
- [ ] Knows when to walk away

## Certification
- Complete all six modules.
- Then pass three independent simulations: introduction, discovery and skeptical customer.
- Each requires **85/100 or higher with no critical errors**. See `03-ai-coaching.md` for scoring and retakes.

Certification permits live outreach once the founders approve readiness. It does not grant independent approval of technical scope, discounts or commitments.
