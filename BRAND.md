# ten forty five — approved brand identity (v1.0)

**Status:** Approved, November 2026
**Direction:** A, refined — The Modern Consultancy with editorial accents
**Full board:** [`brand-mockups/a-refined.html`](brand-mockups/a-refined.html)

## Logo

- **Primary wordmark:** `ten forty five.` — always lowercase, one line, Inter Tight Semibold, tracking −4.5%, with the square full stop.
- **Secondary:** stacked lockup (ten / forty / five) with a steel vertical rule, for tall formats and signage.
- **Compact mark:** lowercase "t" above a progress bar at 72%, on midnight. For avatars, app icons and favicons.
- **Tagline lockup:** wordmark with *we sit next to you, not above you.* set in Instrument Serif Italic underneath.
- **Clear space:** the height of the "t" on all sides. **Minimum width:** 96px on screen, 25mm in print.
- **Never:** capitalise it, put it in italics, add gradients, or stretch or rotate it.

## Typography

| Role | Typeface | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Display and headlines | Inter Tight 500/600 | Tracking −2.5% to −4.5% |
| Body | Inter 400/500 | 15–17px, 1.55 leading |
| Labels and data | IBM Plex Mono 400/500 | 11px, uppercase, +8% tracking |
| Accent | Instrument Serif Italic | One phrase per layout, never a whole headline, never in the logo |

All four are free on Google Fonts.

## Graphic elements

Hairline column rules, progress bars, measuring ticks and the pull quote. Everything aligns to a 12-column grid.

## Rules

1. The wordmark is always lowercase and on one line, except in the stacked lockup.
2. Align everything to the 12-column grid.
3. One headline, one proof point and one action per layout.
4. Steel is an accent and takes up no more than 10% of a layout.
5. Write the way a sharp colleague talks: plainly, with a little wit.
6. Use the serif italic once per layout, for a phrase.

## Photography

Documentary, not staged: owners in their own spaces and teams at real tables, in natural light. Cool white balance (never warmer than 5200K), lifted shadows, space left for type. No handshakes, glowing screens, robots or stock-photo poses.
