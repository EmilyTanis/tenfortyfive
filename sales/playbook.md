# Sales messaging playbook
Workshop 04 | Internal | Scripts and operating suggestions are drafts; commercial rules in brand/messaging.md are approved.

## Roles and target
Angelo researches, qualifies, contacts and books meetings; Emily owns strategy, scope, proposals, pricing and closing in year one. Primary KPI: one qualified meeting per week; revisit after 30 days. Local NJ outbound first; national capability publicly. Start with home services, beauty/wellness and professional services. Look for a visible business problem, decision-maker access, budget fit and interest. Do not assume budget or need from appearance alone.

## Research and qualification
Record business, website, town, dated public source, observed issue, evidence, hypothesis, likely package and unanswered questions in restricted CRM/Drive. Never commit personal prospect data to GitHub. Prioritize genuine need, budget fit, decision access, urgency and ongoing potential. Selective free mockups only for high-value prospects; no speculative work presented as commissioned. No live outreach until founders approve readiness.

## Discovery (draft)
What are you trying to accomplish over the next few months? How do customers find you today? What's working? What takes too much time? What have you tried? What would a useful outcome look like? Who makes the decision? What budget and timing are comfortable? Summarize back; ask permission to recommend a next step. Qualified means real need, decision-maker access, reasonable budget and interest.

## Outreach scripts (draft; personalize and verify)
Initial: Hi [first name], I came across [business] and noticed [specific verified observation]. We help businesses with marketing, websites and technology, and I had an idea that might help with [relevant goal]. Would you be open to a quick conversation? — Angelo, ten forty five
Follow-up 1: Hi [name], just following up on my note about [issue]. One thing worth exploring is [specific idea]. Happy to share what that could look like if useful.
Follow-up 2: Hi [name], I'll leave it here for now. If [goal] becomes a priority, we'd be happy to help. Thanks for your time.
Use accurate sender identity, honor opt-outs, comply with applicable outreach rules including CAN-SPAM for commercial email, and obtain approval before sending. No invented observations or mass spam.

## Recommend the right fit
DIY $19–49 for self-directed buyers (instant checkout). get started. $1,500 when they know the one problem they want solved. think bigger. $3,500 when they know something has to change but not exactly what: a bigger goal, usually several connected problems, starting with a planning step. let's talk. for unusual, extensive or recurring needs. Don't promise specific deliverables in outreach; the deliverable is agreed at scope confirmation. Emily confirms scope, exclusions, timeline, revisions, third-party costs and acceptance criteria before commitment. Fixed price 50/50; no discounts or add-ons without founder decision.

## Objections (draft)
Too expensive: Let's look at what you need most and whether a focused project makes sense. We can also point you toward a self-service resource.
Why not hire someone? If you need ongoing daily support, a hire may be right. We are useful when you need a defined project or specific expertise.
Do you use AI? Yes, where it helps. A real person makes decisions, reviews the work and is accountable for delivery.
Do I own it? You own the paid custom deliverables and your accounts, subject to third-party licensing. No mandatory maintenance.
Can you guarantee sales? No. We agree on goals and measurable deliverables, but results depend on factors beyond our control.

## Handoff and measurement
Send Emily verified research, discovery notes, goal, budget, timing, decision maker, suggested scope and open questions. Track researched, qualified, contacted, replies, meetings booked/held, proposals and wins. Review weekly. Keep client data restricted. Do not send, quote custom pricing or promise timelines without approval.
