# do it yourself. product outlines

Draft for founder review | Workshop 05 decisions 04, 06 and 10 | On sale at the December 15 soft launch.

Each product is a **plain-language guide (PDF, branded from `brand-kit/templates/workbook-cover.html`) plus ready-to-use templates** the buyer copies into their own Google account. Voice: barbecue test. A hairstylist or a landscaper with no marketing background should finish it and have something real to show for it.

Shared rules for all three:
- **One finished result.** Every product ends with something done, not just "learned".
- **Works in an afternoon or a weekend.** No step needs paid software. Where a paid tool helps, it's optional and we say what it costs.
- **No hype or guarantees.** No "10x your leads".
- **Ends with a next step:** "Want us to do it for you? Start a get started. project." (No discount or credit.)
- **Platform-neutral.** Show the options and how to choose; no affiliate links at launch.

---

## 1. build your website. ($19)

**Promise:** plan and write a simple website that tells customers what you do and how to reach you, before you spend money on building it.
**For:** business owners with no website, or one they're embarrassed by.
**Finished result:** a complete one-page website plan with all the words written, ready to paste into any website builder.
**Time:** 2–3 hours.

Guide sections:
1. What your website actually needs to do (answer: what you do, where, how to contact you)
2. Choosing a website builder: Squarespace, Wix, Google Business Profile's free site, and when each makes sense
3. Writing your headline and "what we do" in plain words (with fill-in-the-blank formulas)
4. Photos: what to take on your phone, what to avoid
5. Making it easy to contact you: phone, form, booking, map
6. Getting found on Google: your Google Business Profile and the basics
7. Pre-launch checklist

Templates (3):
- One-page website planner (Google Doc, fill-in-the-blank)
- Copy formulas cheat sheet
- Launch checklist

## 2. market your business. ($29)

**Promise:** a simple 30-day plan to get more people to notice your business, using the channels you already have.
**For:** owners who post sometimes and don't know if it's working.
**Finished result:** a 30-day marketing plan with your first two weeks of posts written and scheduled.
**Time:** a weekend.

Guide sections:
1. Who your best customers are (a one-page customer snapshot)
2. Where those customers already spend time: pick one or two channels, not five
3. What to say: four kinds of posts that work for local businesses (show the work, answer a question, behind the scenes, ask for the review)
4. Getting reviews without feeling awkward (scripts included)
5. Your Google Business Profile as your best free marketing
6. A 30-day plan you can keep up in 30 minutes a day
7. How to tell if it's working: three numbers to check each month

Templates (4):
- Customer snapshot worksheet
- 30-day content calendar (Google Sheet)
- Post-idea bank with 40 fill-in-the-blank prompts
- Review request scripts (text, email, in person)

---

## 3. work smarter. ($49)

**Promise:** find the tasks eating your week and fix the biggest one, with simple tools and AI where it actually helps.
**For:** owners drowning in admin: quotes, scheduling, follow-ups, invoices.
**Finished result:** a list of your biggest time-wasters, ranked, with the top one automated or simplified.
**Time:** a weekend.

Guide sections:
1. Track one normal week: where your time actually goes
2. Spot the repeat work: the five most common time-wasters for small businesses
3. Pick the one worth fixing first (time saved vs. effort)
4. Fix it simply: templates, saved replies, online booking, automatic reminders
5. Using AI safely and usefully: drafting emails, quotes and posts, with a person always checking (and what never to paste into an AI tool)
6. Step-by-step setups for three common fixes: instant reply to quote requests, booking with automatic reminders, invoice follow-up
7. Keeping it running: a 15-minute monthly check

Templates (4):
- Weekly time tracker (Google Sheet)
- Time-waster scorecard: ranks tasks by hours and effort to fix
- Saved-reply library: 20 ready-to-edit messages
- AI prompt starter pack for small-business admin

**Why it's the $49 product:** it saves the most time and includes the step-by-step setups. It's also the closest match to our human-led, AI-enabled positioning.
