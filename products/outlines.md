# do it yourself. product outlines

Draft v2 for founder review | Workshop 05 decisions 04, 06 and 10 | On sale at the December 15 soft launch.

Each product is a **plain-language guide (PDF, branded from `brand-kit/templates/workbook-cover.html`) plus ready-to-use templates** the buyer copies into their own Google account. Voice: barbecue test. A hairstylist or a landscaper with no marketing background should finish it and have something real to show for it.

## The angle: how to do it today

These are not "how to build a website" guides from five years ago. Each one shows how to do the job **faster and better with today's tools**: what a small business can do now that it couldn't before, and where a person still matters most. This is the reason to buy them over a free blog post, and it matches how we position ten forty five: marketing for 2026, not ten years ago.

Shared rules for all three:
- **Then vs. now.** Every section has a short box: how this used to be done, and how you can do it now.
- **Being found by AI, not just Google.** Customers now ask AI assistants and read AI answers in search ("who's a good landscaper near me?"). Each product covers what that means for its topic.
- **AI with a human in charge.** AI drafts; you decide, check facts and make it sound like you. Never paste private customer details into an AI tool.
- **Approach vs. tools.** The guide teaches an approach that lasts. A separate, dated **"Tools right now"** page names current options and costs. It's refreshed quarterly, and buyers get updates free.
- **One finished result.** Every product ends with something done, not just "learned".
- **An afternoon or a weekend.** No step needs paid software; where a paid tool helps, it's optional and the cost is stated.
- **No hype or guarantees.** No "10x your leads".
- **Platform-neutral.** Show the options and how to choose; no affiliate links at launch.
- **Ends with a next step:** "Want us to do it for you? Start a get started. project." (No discount or credit.)

## 1. build your website. ($19)

**Promise:** get a simple, good-looking website live in an afternoon, using today's AI-assisted builders, without it looking like everyone else's.
**For:** business owners with no website, or one they're embarrassed by.
**Finished result:** a live one-page website with your own words, real photos, a way to contact or book you, and a Google Business Profile that matches.
**Time:** an afternoon.

**Then vs. now:** five years ago this meant hiring a designer or fighting a template for a weekend. Now most website builders can draft a whole site from a few sentences about your business. The work that matters has shifted: giving the AI the right information, then fixing what it gets wrong.

Guide sections:
1. What your website actually needs to do: say what you do, where, and how to reach you, in five seconds
2. Choosing a builder in 2026: AI-assisted builders, booking or payments built in, and what you'll pay
3. The 15-minute business brief: the information that makes an AI-built site sound like you, not generic
4. Let AI draft it, then edit like a pro: what to keep, what to cut, the five mistakes AI sites always make
5. Photos: shoot on your phone; what AI editing is fine (lighting, background cleanup) and what isn't (fake work)
6. Let customers act right away: tap-to-call, text, online booking, instant quote requests
7. Getting found: Google Business Profile, and what helps AI assistants recommend you (clear service area, reviews, consistent details everywhere)
8. Launch checklist

Templates (4):
- 15-minute business brief (Google Doc), which doubles as the input for any AI builder
- AI prompt pack for website wording, with "make it sound like me" edits
- Website edit checklist: fix what AI gets wrong
- Launch and "found online" checklist

## 2. market your business. ($29)

**Promise:** find the way of marketing that fits your business and run it for 30 days, using today's tools to do in minutes what used to take hours.
**For:** owners who post sometimes, or not at all, and don't know what's working.
**Finished result:** your chosen marketing path, a 30-day plan for it, and the first two weeks of content or outreach ready to go.
**Time:** a weekend.

**Then vs. now:** marketing used to mean "post on every platform" or "buy an ad and hope". Now short video, messaging apps, online reviews and AI answers often matter more than a polished post. Small businesses can create, schedule and measure in minutes. The hard part is picking the right path, not doing everything.

**Choose your path.** A short self-quiz at the start sorts the buyer into one of four paths. Every path shares the basics, then goes deeper on its own approach:

| Path | Best for | How it works today |
|---|---|---|
| **Be seen** (social-first) | Visual and personality-driven businesses: salons, food, makers | Short video, behind-the-scenes, regular posting; social platforms as search engines |
| **Get the inquiry** (lead capture) | Service businesses that quote: trades, cleaning, landscaping | A simple landing page, instant replies, quote forms, local search ads |
| **Sell directly** | Businesses with products or bookable services | Shoppable posts, selling in DMs and messaging, simple online checkout, text offers |
| **Be recommended** (reviews and referrals) | Trust-based local businesses: health, professional services | Reviews everywhere, Google Business Profile, referral asks, showing up in AI answers |

Guide sections:
1. The quiz: which path fits your business, your customers and your time
2. Who your best customers are: a one-page snapshot
3. The basics every path needs: Google Business Profile, reviews, one clear offer
4. Your path in depth: the playbook for the chosen path, how it works in 2026, with examples
5. Making content and messages fast: AI to brainstorm, draft and repurpose (one video becomes five posts), you to approve
6. Your 30-day plan, built to take 30 minutes a day
7. Is it working? The three numbers to watch for your path

Templates (5):
- Path quiz and customer snapshot
- 30-day plan (Google Sheet), one tab per path
- 40 content and outreach prompts, sorted by path
- AI prompt pack for posts, captions, replies and repurposing
- Review and referral request scripts (text, email, in person)

## 3. work smarter. ($49)

**Promise:** find the tasks eating your week and fix the biggest one with automation and AI tools that weren't affordable for small businesses five years ago.
**For:** owners drowning in admin: quotes, scheduling, follow-ups, invoices, messages.
**Finished result:** your biggest time-wasters, ranked, with the top one automated or handed to an AI-assisted tool and checked by you.
**Time:** a weekend.

**Then vs. now:** automation used to need a developer or expensive software. Now the scheduling, invoicing and email tools a small business already pays for can do much of this, and AI assistants can draft quotes, replies and summaries in seconds. You still decide what goes out, and you never hand over anything you'd regret.

Guide sections:
1. Track one normal week: where your time actually goes
2. The repeat work: the five most common time-wasters for small businesses
3. Pick the one worth fixing first (time saved vs. effort)
4. What's possible now: automations, AI assistants and "set it once" tools, in plain words
5. AI safely: what it's great at, where it makes mistakes, what never to paste in, and always checking before it reaches a customer
6. Step-by-step setups for three common fixes: instant reply to quote requests, booking with automatic reminders, invoice follow-up
7. Keeping it running: a 15-minute monthly check

Templates (4):
- Weekly time tracker (Google Sheet)
- Time-waster scorecard: ranks tasks by hours and effort to fix
- Saved-reply library: 20 ready-to-edit messages
- AI prompt pack for small-business admin (quotes, replies, summaries, policies)

**Why it's the $49 product:** it saves the most time, includes step-by-step setups, and is the closest match to our human-led, AI-enabled positioning.
