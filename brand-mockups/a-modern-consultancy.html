07 / Photography & art direction

Real rooms, real work, natural light.

Documentary over staged. Owners at their own counters, teams around real tables, hands on actual work. Cool white balance, lifted shadows, generous negative space for type. No handshakes, no glowing screens.

15 / Usage guidelines

Keep it calm. Keep it aligned.

The identity works because of what it leaves out. These rules protect that.

  • 01The wordmark is always lowercase and always set as one line, except in the stacked lockup.
  • 02Align everything to the 12-column grid. If it floats, it's wrong.
  • 03One headline, one proof point, one action per layout.
  • 04Steel is an accent: never more than 10% of a layout.
  • 05Voice: say it like a smart colleague would, plainly and with a little wit.
