15 / Usage guidelines

Rules of the system.

A modular identity only works if the modules are respected. These rules keep every touchpoint consistent and quick to produce.

Rule 01 · Grid

Everything snaps to the 32px module. Margins, image crops and type baselines all sit on it.

Rule 02 · One diagonal

45° is the only angle allowed, and it appears once per layout at most. Everything else is orthogonal.

Rule 03 · Tech, lightly

Show outcomes (time saved, steps removed), never machinery. No robots, glow, circuitry or neon.
