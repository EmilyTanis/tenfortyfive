Recommendation

Following the brief's starting preference, lead with A · The Modern Consultancy as the core system (wordmark, grid and palette) and borrow B's serif-italic moments and statement-led social templates to add personality. C's 10:45 clock mark and "resolve" device are worth keeping in reserve for the automation service line and digital products.
