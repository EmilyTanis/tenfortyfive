TypographySay it big. Then say it well.

Oversized grotesk headlines create the impact. Serif italics add nuance, asides and emphasis, and should be used no more than once per layout.

Marketing is a business problem wearing a nice outfit.

Body copy is set at 16–18px with comfortable 1.55 leading. Write in short paragraphs, as if explaining it over coffee.

Photography & art directionPortraits, not poses.

Shoot like a magazine profile: people who own businesses, photographed with character in their own spaces. Direct gaze, crops with tension, desaturated cool grade. Words lead; images support.

The wide
Team at work, shot through a doorway: observed, not staged

Tonal studies shown. Grade: −20 saturation, cool shadows, retained skin warmth. Never: laptops with glowing screens, handshakes, people pointing at whiteboards.

15

Usage guidelinesHouse style.

Editorial doesn't mean decorative. Every layout should still pass the test: would a busy business owner understand this in three seconds?

✓ Doten forty five
✕ All groteskten forty five
✕ Wrong word italicten forty five
✕ Title caseTen Forty Five
Layout

Asymmetric, never random. Hang headlines from the 12-column grid, leave at least one third of each layout empty, and use one serif italic moment per composition.

Voice

We say: "Your ads aren't the problem." We don't say: "Leverage synergistic omnichannel solutions." Witty, never snarky. Clever only when it makes things clearer.
