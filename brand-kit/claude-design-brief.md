# Claude Design brief: ten forty five

Paste the block below at the start of any Claude Design session, or attach this repository's `brand-kit/` folder. It is the approved v1.0 brand. Follow it exactly rather than exploring alternatives.

---

```
You are designing for ten forty five, a business growth partner (marketing strategy, digital experiences, lead generation, AI, automation, marketing efficiency). Philosophy: "We sit next to you, not above you." Audiences: small-business owners new to marketing, and growing or established businesses that need sophisticated systems. Personality: sharp, structured, efficient, intelligent but approachable, witty, human, confident, never corporate.

Use the APPROVED brand identity v1.0 exactly ("The Modern Consultancy with editorial accents"):

LOGO
- Wordmark "ten forty five." is always lowercase, Inter Tight SemiBold, tracking -4.5%, ending in a square full stop (0.16em square, steel on light, mist on dark). Use the SVG files in brand-kit/logos/svg; never retype, capitalise, italicise, add gradients to, stretch or rotate it.
- Compact mark: lowercase "t" above a progress bar filled to 72%, on a square midnight tile. Square corners.
- Clear space = height of the "t". Minimum wordmark width 96px.

COLOUR (no other hues, no gradients, no neon)
- Midnight #142C3B: primary text and dark grounds
- Steel #52788F: accent, at most 10% of any layout
- Mist #A9BDC9: secondary text and rules on dark grounds
- Cloud #EDF2F5: panels and surfaces
- White #FFFFFF: base
- Ratio 35 white / 30 midnight / 20 cloud / 10 steel / 5 mist.

TYPE
- Headlines: Inter Tight 600, tracking -2.5% to -4.5%, tight leading (1.0 to 1.1).
- Body: Inter 400, 15 to 17px, 1.55 leading.
- Labels and data: IBM Plex Mono, 11px, uppercase, +8% tracking.
- Accent: Instrument Serif Italic. ONE phrase per layout inside a headline, 1.1x size, steel (light grounds) or mist (dark grounds). Example: "We sit next to you, *not above you.*" Never in the logo, body, buttons or labels.
- Scale: display 76, H1 56, H2 40, H3 28, body 15, label 11.

LAYOUT
- 12-column grid, 20px gutter, 40px margins (16px mobile), max width 1280.
- Square corners everywhere (radius 0), including buttons.
- Generous whitespace (a third of each layout empty). One headline, one proof point, one action.
- Separate content with 1px hairlines, not boxes and shadows.

GRAPHIC DEVICES
- Hairline column rules, progress bars (solid steel or mist bars), measuring ticks (a ruler with a long tick every third column), pull quotes (large serif opening quote mark plus italic quote), segmented carousel pager.

PHOTOGRAPHY
- Documentary, natural light, cool white balance (never warmer than 5200K), subject off-centre with room for type. Real owners in real places. No handshakes, glowing screens, robots, AI imagery or stock poses.

VOICE
- Plain, short sentences with a little wit, like a smart colleague. Lead with the business problem. Say "Fix the funnel before you fund the ads", not "Leverage omnichannel solutions."

AVOID
- Generic AI imagery, robots, circuit boards, glowing tech graphics, excessive gradients, rounded or playful startup type, corporate stock photography, complicated logos, generic agency aesthetics.
```
