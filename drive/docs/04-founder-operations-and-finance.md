## 04 — Founder Operations & Finance \| Working draft

# Role ownership

Creative/operations founder: strategy, design, websites, AI, delivery and QA. Growth/finance founder: paid media, sales, customer service, reporting, billing and bookkeeping. Both approve major spending and pricing exceptions.

# Capacity

15 hours each weekly; reserve 40% for administration and sales initially. Cap concurrent builds at one until delivery hours are known.

# Client workflow

Qualify, scope, agreement, deposit, kickoff, weekly status, QA, approval, handoff, final invoice. Use client-owned accounts and least-privilege access.

# Finance

Separate funds, track expenses and collections, exclude client media spend from revenue, agree tax reserves with accountant.

# Founder agreement

Decide ownership, contributions, authority, compensation, distributions, IP, deadlock and exit/buyout terms with legal advice.

# Risk controls

Check employer policies; use independent equipment and accounts. Review privacy, security, insurance and contracts.

# Growth gates

Track collected revenue, profit, recurring revenue and hours. Consider leaving employment only after sustained profitability and cash reserves.
