# 01\. Master brand brief

Act as a senior creative director and brand identity designer developing a premium, contemporary brand identity for a new marketing consultancy called ten forty five.

## About the business

Ten Forty Five is a business growth partner specializing in marketing strategy, digital experiences, lead generation, AI, automation, and marketing efficiency.  
We approach businesses from the perspective of an in-house team rather than a traditional external agency. We understand the business problem first, then develop and execute the marketing solution.  
Our philosophy: We sit next to you, not above you.  
We have two primary audiences: small-business owners and entrepreneurs with little marketing experience who need affordable, easy-to-use digital resources; and growing and established businesses that need sophisticated, customized marketing strategies, systems, and solutions.

## Brand personality

Sharp, structured, and efficient; intelligent but approachable; witty, natural, and human; clear and confident; fresh and contemporary; professional without being overly corporate. Imagine a highly capable business partner who is also someone everyone wants in the room.

## Visual direction

The primary wordmark must read “ten forty five” in lowercase. Prioritize sharp, structured, contemporary typography. Use calm steel blue, white, and complementary cool-toned neutrals. Explore strong typographic hierarchy, intentional whitespace, and structured grid systems. The identity should feel like a modern consultancy rather than a traditional advertising agency. It must stand out in digital advertising and social media without relying on loud colors or visual clutter.

## Avoid

Generic AI imagery; robots, circuit boards, and glowing technology graphics; excessive gradients; rounded, playful startup typography; traditional corporate stock photography; overly complicated logos; generic marketing agency aesthetics.

## Deliverables

Create a comprehensive visual brand identity presentation featuring:  
1\. Primary lowercase wordmark  
2\. Secondary logo and compact brand mark  
3\. Logo variations on light and dark backgrounds  
4\. Color palette with HEX codes  
5\. Primary and secondary typography  
6\. Graphic elements and patterns  
7\. Photography and art direction  
8\. Business card mockups  
9\. Instagram educational carousel templates

# 02\. Creative direction A — The Modern Consultancy

Using the Ten Forty Five master brand brief, develop a creative direction titled “The Modern Consultancy.”  
Draw inspiration from contemporary strategy consultancies, Swiss graphic design, and premium professional services firms.  
Use a muted steel-blue palette, crisp white backgrounds, deep midnight blue, structured grids, and confident lowercase typography.  
The design should communicate business intelligence, credibility, clarity, and efficiency.  
Explore subtle graphic devices inspired by alignment, organization, progress, and precision. The typography should be the hero.  
Make the brand feel established enough to work with sophisticated businesses but approachable enough for a local small-business owner.  
Create the complete branding kit and present it as a professional agency brand identity board.

# 03\. Creative direction B — Editorial Intelligence

Using the Ten Forty Five master brand brief, develop a creative direction titled “Editorial Intelligence.”  
Create a sophisticated, design-forward identity inspired by contemporary editorial publications, independent design studios, and premium lifestyle brands.

Create the complete branding kit and present it as a cohesive editorial-style brand presentation.

# 04\. Creative direction C — Structured Systems

Using the Ten Forty Five master brand brief, develop a creative direction titled “Structured Systems.”  
Create a modern identity that visually communicates efficiency, organization, intelligent systems, and business transformation.  
Explore modular layouts, geometric graphic elements, structured typography, subtle grid patterns, and visual representations of simplifying complexity.  
Use a calm steel-blue palette with white and deep blue accents.
