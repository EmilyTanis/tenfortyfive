## Brand identity & creative direction \| V1 — for founder review

# Brand foundation

Independent digital consultancy combining marketing strategy, digital experiences, paid media and intelligent automation. Audience: established small businesses first, with room for midsize companies and startups. Personality: intelligent, intentional, confident, accessible.

# Founding philosophy

1 — Lead with purpose. 0 — Think without limitations. 4 — Build with intention. 5 — Embrace evolution. These are chosen principles, not objective numerological meanings. Keep the residential origin private.

# Wordmark system

Recommended: lowercase ten forty five. in a medium-weight geometric or neo-grotesk sans serif. Secondary mark: 1045, for compact applications. Explore a subtle accent on the period. No final logo artwork yet.

# Visual routes

A — Architectural: ink \#171918, warm ivory \#F4F2EC, muted olive \#747D68. B — Digital precision: ink \#151923, white \#F7F8FA, cobalt \#3157E8. C — Modern editorial: espresso \#302722, cream \#F5F0E8, rust \#A64B35. Recommend A for broad consultancy positioning; B if technology should lead.

# Typography

Proposed: Manrope for headlines and Inter for body text; both are available under open font licenses, verify license before distribution. Alternative: a restrained serif accent for editorial headlines. Use generous whitespace, strong alignment and simple grids.

# Messaging hierarchy

Name: ten forty five. Descriptor: Independent digital consultancy. Tagline: Grounded in strategy. Driven by possibility. Homepage headline: Better ways to grow, build and operate. Supporting copy: We bring marketing, digital experiences and intelligent automation together to help businesses move forward.

# Voice and imagery

Direct, specific, confident, approachable. Avoid hype, jargon and guarantees. Use real work, interface details, architectural textures and simple diagrams; avoid generic stock imagery and robot graphics.

# Founder decisions

Choose visual route A/B/C; wordmark with or without period; technology-forward versus balanced positioning; preferred descriptor; whether founders appear prominently; whether to use a short or longer tagline.
