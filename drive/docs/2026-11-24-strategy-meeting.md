TEN FORTY FIVE — STRATEGY MEETING  
November 24, 2026  
Founders: Emily and Angelo

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY  
Ten Forty Five will launch as a modern marketing consultancy and growth partner with a practical blend of strategy, digital experiences, and AI-enabled business efficiency. The immediate priority is generating paying clients while building a credible portfolio and repeatable systems. Both founders can devote approximately 15 hours weekly; initial launch budget is \$500–\$1,000. The long-term goal is to replace a salary and eventually support both founders full-time.

BRAND AND POSITIONING  
Name: Ten Forty Five; lowercase “ten forty five” for the wordmark, inspired by house number 1045\. Professional name in copy: Ten Forty Five. Position as an approachable, responsive consultancy with in-house thinking without the overhead; collaborative philosophy: “We sit next to you, not above you.” These phrases are positioning candidates, not finalized taglines. Visual direction: minimal, structured geometric typography and whitespace; steel blue \#427EAB, ice white \#F2F7FA, midnight \#142C3B, and white. Warm, clear language with subtle personality; avoid jargon, generic tech aesthetics, and exaggerated promises.

AUDIENCE AND REVENUE MODEL  
Serve local small businesses and entrepreneurs initially, while keeping the agency open to startups and midsize organizations. Do not commit to an industry niche yet: content and market intelligence should examine broad small-business needs, then segment by observed opportunity. One unified brand and website will support two entry paths: “I want to do this myself” for downloadable tools and “I want expert help” for consulting. Consulting is the more immediate revenue priority; digital products are a longer-term scalable channel.

SERVICES AND POTENTIAL OFFERS  
Three service areas: Strategy & Growth (assessments, roadmaps, consulting); Digital Experiences (simple, editable 3–5-page websites, landing pages, lead capture); Automation & Efficiency (process audits, workflow automation, reporting, data cleanup, practical AI tools and handoff training). Offer fixed-scope packages for clear needs and custom consulting for complex projects. Potential packages: Growth Blueprint (paid diagnostic and 30–90-day priorities), Marketing Fix (targeted implementation), and Growth Partnership (optional recurring support). Early exploratory figures, not approved prices: founding-client Blueprint \$395–\$595 and small websites \$1,500–\$2,500. Keep free discovery distinct from paid strategy; validate margins, time, and demand before setting prices.

PORTFOLIO  
Build and launch a real website with quote capture for Emily’s father’s landscaping business, with permission to document genuine outcomes. Potential vintage merchandise and beauty/barbershop projects can be clearly labeled concepts until launched. Do not claim fabricated results or take on unlimited unpaid projects.

DIGITAL PRODUCTS  
Start with one useful standalone downloadable resource for owners with little marketing experience, potentially a Marketing Efficiency Toolkit with a self-assessment, task inventory, and prioritized next steps. Exploratory price \$29–\$49, to be validated. Use free content to introduce the resource and provide an option to move into consulting.

FOUNDER RESPONSIBILITIES — YEAR ONE  
Angelo: business development and SDR lead; owns local prospect research, warm and personalized email/social outreach, CRM, lead qualification, follow-ups, booking, publishing calendar, community engagement, and lead handoffs. His initial qualification calls should cover business, problem, desired outcome, urgency, buying path, and existing tools. Do not promise technical scope, price, results, or timelines without Emily’s review. Every qualified handoff should include a brief, need, and agreed next step.  
Emily: strategy, deeper discovery, solution design, pricing, proposals, presentations, closing, technical execution, AI content strategy and approval of technical claims. Angelo may shadow and gradually expand closing responsibility later.

LEAD GENERATION AND SALES SYSTEM  
Year-one prospecting begins with local small businesses, warm introductions, and personalized digital outreach rather than national campaigns or high-volume blasting. Angelo prefers email and social media, not in-person cold outreach or calls as the initial channel. Launch agency-branded email, LinkedIn company page, Instagram business account, Facebook business page, a simple CRM (HubSpot free tier is a candidate), and scheduling. Angelo should use his own professional LinkedIn profile for person-to-person outreach, clearly identifying his agency role; agency Instagram/Facebook accounts handle branded engagement. The founders can appear occasionally in content without becoming the entire brand. A low-friction “Work With Us” form should ask only essential details and immediately offer booking, with lead capture into the CRM.

PROPOSED 15-HOUR BUSINESS DEVELOPMENT CADENCE  
Monday: research 10–15 local prospects. Tuesday: personalized outreach and follow-up. Wednesday: qualification calls and scheduling. Thursday: outreach, community engagement, follow-up. Friday: CRM hygiene, handoffs, and review. Initial testing targets: 15–20 personalized outreach attempts and approximately two qualified introductory calls booked weekly; these are hypotheses, not guarantees. Measure responses, qualification, meetings, proposals, and won revenue.

SOCIAL CHANNELS AS SALES PIPELINE  
LinkedIn: expertise, professional outreach, consulting leads. Instagram: educational examples and practical guidance linked to offers. Facebook: local relationships and relevant business communities. Organize content to attract, educate, and convert. Measure visits, downloads, bookings, and revenue rather than followers alone. Angelo owns production and publishing; Emily sets strategy and checks substantive claims.

AI-ASSISTED MARKET INTELLIGENCE AND CONTENT ENGINE  
Build a hybrid system using existing tools first (AI assistant, shared content database/calendar, Canva, scheduling platform), with optional future Claude Code/GitHub automation. Weekly batch workflow: research target-audience challenges and recent, credible sources; analyze broad small-business needs and emerging patterns; recommend audience-specific topics with sources and conversion goals; draft channel-appropriate posts; human review; schedule approved content. On-demand workflow: Angelo or Emily inputs a campaign, trend, client question, or new offer and receives a coordinated content set. Repurpose strong ideas across channels. Feed anonymized patterns from sales conversations into the research process. Distinguish observed evidence from assumptions, preserve source links, and never auto-publish without approval. Track content-production time saved as a possible future case study.
