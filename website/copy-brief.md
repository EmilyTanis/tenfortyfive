# Website copy brief
Workshop 04 | Draft copy and proposed architecture for Workshop 05 review. Approved brand/commercial rules: brand/messaging.md.

## Goals
Visitors understand what we do, why we exist, what it costs and how to start. Company-led public identity; no founder photos/names at launch without approval. Mobile-first, accessible, plain language. No fabricated proof.

## Proposed navigation
Home / What we do / Pricing / Work / About / Resources / Contact. Shop and checkout paths for digital products and fixed projects. Final sitemap pending.

## Home draft
Hero: your business. our expertise.
Marketing & technology consultancy. We help businesses reach more customers, build better websites and make smarter use of technology — without the need for a full-time hire or long-term agency commitment.
Primary CTA: Find the right help. Secondary: See our work.
Needs section: Getting started? Looking for more customers? Know things could be better? Need some time back?
Capabilities: Web & digital experiences — websites that make it easier for customers to find and contact you. Marketing & growth — help people discover your business and choose you. AI & business systems — make everyday work easier with technology that fits your business.
How it works: Tell us what you need. Agree on the work and price. We do the work. You own the result (subject to third-party licenses).

## About draft
We started ten forty five because we saw a gap in how businesses access marketing and technology expertise. Hiring a full-time team isn't always practical, and a long-term agency commitment isn't always necessary. We wanted to create another option: straightforward, flexible and built around what your business actually needs. Human-led, AI-enabled.

## Work and trust
Use approved real portfolio work with permission; clearly label concept projects. Show problem, approach and deliverables; report outcomes only if verified. No invented logos, testimonials or metrics. Explain direct communication and ownership.

## Customer journeys and launch checks
DIY: browse → product details → checkout → delivery. Fixed: choose package → scope questionnaire → confirmation/payment flow → kickoff → delivery and balance → handoff. Custom: discovery → proposal → agreement. Exact checkout sequencing and refund terms require approval. Test mobile, keyboard access, forms, links, payment sandbox, privacy, terms, analytics consent and error states. Do not launch or deploy without founder approval. Visual assets must come from approved kit, not inferred palette.
