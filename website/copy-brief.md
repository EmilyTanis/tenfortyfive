# Website copy brief
Workshop 05 | Structure approved in [docs/workshop-05.md](../docs/workshop-05.md); copy below is draft. Brand and commercial rules: [brand/messaging.md](../brand/messaging.md). Visuals: [brand-kit/](../brand-kit/).

## Goals
Inform, then sell. Early visitors arrive from ads and personal LinkedIn outreach, wanting to know what we do and what it costs, not ready to buy. Every page answers *what do you do, what does it cost, how do I start*. Primary action: **Book a free call**. Secondary: **See pricing**. Company-led public identity; no founder names or photos. Mobile-first, accessible, plain language (barbecue test). No fabricated proof.

## No gates
No pop-ups, exit-intent modals or self-opening chat. Pricing, package details, process and FAQ readable without a form. Booking asks for name, email and one optional "what are you working on?" field. Outreach and ad links land on the page that answers what the message promised. Cookie consent only as legally required, as a small bar.

## Navigation (soft launch)
Home / What we do / Pricing / About / Book a call. Footer: Privacy, Terms. Unlisted: one landing page per ad campaign. Later: Work (once the landscaping project is live), Shop / Resources (if the product range grows beyond the Pricing page).

## Home draft
Order: value first, price second.
1. **Opening.** your business. our expertise. — We help businesses reach more customers, build better websites and make smarter use of technology, without a full-time hire or a long-term agency contract. CTA: Book a free call. Small link: see pricing.
2. **What you get.** More customers finding you. A website that does its job. Time back from busywork. A clear plan for what to do next.
3. **What we do.** Websites — easy for customers to find, use on their phone and contact you from, and easy for you to update. Marketing — help the right people discover your business and choose you. AI & business systems — make everyday work easier with technology that fits how you already work. (2–3 concrete examples each; draft until confirmed.)
4. **How we work.** Tell us what you need. Agree on the work and price. We do the work. You own the result (subject to third-party licenses). Plain language, quick replies, real people making the decisions with AI helping.
5. **Why ten forty five.** You own everything. No long contracts. Clear prices up front.
6. **Pricing.** One compact row: do it yourself. from $19 · get started. $1,500 · think bigger. $3,500 · let's talk. custom. Link: See pricing.
7. **Closing.** Book a free call.

No testimonials or client logos until real, permitted proof exists. "Recent work" (landscaping project) added once live and measured.

## Pricing draft
- **do it yourself.** From $19. A little direction goes a long way. Practical guides and templates for business owners who'd rather do it themselves. Three products: build your website. $19 · market your business. $29 · work smarter. $49. CTA: Buy (instant checkout, Apple Pay / Google Pay). 14-day refund if it's not useful.
- **get started.** $1,500. One thing. Done well. Solve one problem: we agree the one thing, then get straight to work. Typically 2–3 weeks · 2 rounds of changes · 7 days of support. CTA: Start a get started. project.
- **think bigger.** $3,500. More room to make things happen. Reach a bigger goal: we start with a plan, then build the pieces to work together. Typically 3–5 weeks · 3 rounds of changes · 30 days of support plus a check-in. CTA: Start a think bigger. project.
- **let's talk.** Custom pricing. Not everything fits in a box. For unusual projects, bigger challenges or ongoing help. CTA: Book a free call.

How to choose: I know what I need → get started. · Something has to change, but I'm not sure what → think bigger. · A lot, or ongoing help → let's talk.

Every project includes: kickoff, updates as often as you'd like, set rounds of changes, handoff with a recorded walkthrough, support after launch, and you own everything once it's paid.

How payment works: tell us about your project (four quick questions) or book a free call. Within one business day we send the confirmed scope, timeline and a payment link. Half to start, half before handoff. No subscriptions. Outside costs (hosting, ad spend, software) listed before you commit.

FAQ topics: what fits in each package; what if my project is bigger; do I own the work; do you use AI; can you guarantee results (no); do I need a call first (no).

## About draft
We started ten forty five because we saw a gap in how businesses access marketing and technology expertise. Hiring a full-time team isn't always practical, and a long-term agency commitment isn't always necessary. We wanted to create another option: straightforward, flexible and built around what your business actually needs. Human-led, AI-enabled.

## Work and trust
Use approved real work with permission. Show problem, approach and result; report outcomes only after measuring against a baseline. Do not describe unpaid work as a paid client. No concept projects at launch. No invented logos, testimonials or metrics.

## Customer journeys and launch checks
- **do it yourself.:** Pricing → Buy → Stripe checkout → download page + email with link.
- **Fixed projects:** Pricing → project form (4 questions) → confirmation email with scope, timeline and payment link → first payment → welcome email and kickoff → work → second payment → handoff.
- **Free call:** Book a call → call → same confirmation email → as above. let's talk. → proposal → agreement.

Test before launch: mobile, keyboard access, forms, booking, Stripe test purchases and refunds, Apple Pay on iPhone, links, Privacy and Terms, analytics consent, error states. Do not launch or deploy without founder approval.
